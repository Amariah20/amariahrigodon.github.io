A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Where business thinking meets technology.

I'm a Seychellois technology professional based in Dublin, Ireland. Having lived and worked across Seychelles, the United Kingdom and Ireland, I've developed an international perspective on business, technology and problem-solving.

My experience spans government digital transformation, technology consulting, software development and entrepreneurship. I combine business understanding with hands-on technical expertise, allowing me to bridge the gap between business needs and technology solutions.

I hold a First Class Honours BSc in Computer Science with Business and I'm currently completing an MBA in Cloud Computing. I'm particularly interested in technology consulting, solutions architecture and solution engineering, where I can combine my technical background with my passion for solving business challenges.

MY APPROACH

How I solve problems.

I combine stakeholder insight, observation and process analysis with practical solution design to address the underlying business need.

01

Investigate

Listen to stakeholders and observe how work really happens. Compare the current and desired states to uncover root causes and clarify the real objective.

02

Analyse & Design

Identify process gaps and improvement opportunities, then translate findings into clear requirements and well-reasoned solution recommendations.

03

Collaborate & Deliver

Partner with business and technical teams to build, test and introduce solutions that address the agreed need.

04

Measure & Improve

Evaluate results, support adoption and use feedback to make ongoing improvements that deliver lasting value.
